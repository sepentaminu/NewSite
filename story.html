Story

The Fall of Solas

In a time of myth and legend, the kingdom of Solas was a beacon of light. But darkness coveted this light, and the Shadow Lord, Malakor, cast his long shadow over the land. The kingdom fell, its light extinguished, and its people scattered.

A Glimmer of Hope

But even in the darkest of nights, a single star can shine. A prophecy speaks of a hero who will rise from the ashes, a descendant of the royal bloodline, who will wield the legendary Sunstone and reclaim the throne. You are that hero.

Your Journey Begins

Your journey will be fraught with peril. You will face fearsome beasts, navigate treacherous dungeons, and confront the Shadow Lord's most powerful minions. But you will not be alone. You will gather allies, forge powerful weapons, and uncover the secrets of the past.
